## Description (1712/4000)

Rooms is a small, private place for you and your friends to keep track of everything you watch, read and play, and to see what everyone else made of it.

ONE LOG FOR ALL FOUR
Films, TV, books and games, side by side. Rate in half stars, say what you loved about it, add a line if you want one. Your diary keeps every night in, in order, and your rankings keep your favourites in the order you'd defend.

YOUR ROOM
Make a room with the people whose taste you trust, up to three rooms each. See what they're in the middle of, what they just finished and what they thought. React, reply, and ask the room for something short and tense for Friday.

FEED
A ring for everyone who's logged something in the last two days. Tap it to see what they finished, what they gave it and what they said, one card at a time, then reply or add it to your list. Below: the people you follow, then what everyone else is finishing.

PICK SOMETHING TOGETHER
Plan a watch night: swipe through options, match on the ones you all want, and vote. Rooms suggests things that suit all of you, not just one person, and tells you which ones are on your streaming services.

PICKS FROM PEOPLE WHO RATE LIKE YOU
Suggestions come from friends whose scores move with yours, from the films and books you loved, and from the directors and authors you keep coming back to. It learns from what you rated low too, and it says why it picked each one.

GAMES FOR THE ROOM
A weekly question, a monthly bracket, room charts, hot takes, and your year in review when December comes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Your rooms are yours. Report and block are always one tap away, and you can download everything or close your account from Settings.

## What's new in this version
(1.0 said: "The first version of Rooms on the App Store." This is for 1.0.1.)

Stacks: make a stack of films, shows, books or games, find everyone else's, and save the ones you like. Filter by genre, search inside them, or turn one into a watch night with your room.

Five Clues: a new puzzle every day. Guess the film, show, book or game in as few clues as you can, keep a streak going, and see how your room's doing this week.

Party games for your room: Fake Blurb, Bad Pitch, Title Mash, Who Rated It? and Hot Take Court. Everyone plays on their own phone, in their own time.

Also: Five Clues on your home-screen widget, a simpler way to say what you loved, quicker ranking, and new tab icons.

## App Review Information
- **Sign-in required:** yes. Give them a demo account (email and password) with a room, a few
  friends and some entries in it. Apple won't make one, and an app they can't get into is rejected.
- **Notes for the reviewer** (paste, then fill in the account):

> Rooms is a private log of films, TV, books and games, shared with small rooms of friends.
> Sign in with the demo account above. It is in a room called Movie Night with two test
> accounts (demo_mia and demo_tom) who have logged a few things, and it follows them, so the
> room, the Feed tab and the suggestions have something in them.
> Terms of use (no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users) are agreed to on the
> sign-in screen, and asked for once on first launch: https://dontdateleos.github.io/Rooms/terms.html
> Reporting and blocking: open anyone's entry (tap it) or profile, and both are at the bottom:
> "Report this" and "Block". Blocks are undone from Settings → Blocked (tap your name at the top of the app, then the cog).
> Deleting an account: tap your name at the top, then Settings (the cog) → Close your account.
> Notifications are optional and only sent for things in your own rooms.
